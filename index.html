Full disclaimer & real-world help

About the game: The Family Dog is a fictional slapstick game built around an inside joke and meant as stress relief. It depicts exaggerated, player-triggered violence toward a cartoon dog. The dog can fight back and knock the player down, so the gameplay is not one-sided. It is all for laughs and giggles; no real animals were used or harmed, and the joke is never real animal suffering.

My position: I do not support or excuse animal abuse. Real abuse and neglect are cruel and unacceptable. People who abuse animals are genuine assholes; I consider them subhuman and think they deserve to go to an electric chair.

If you see animal abuse in real life: Contact local animal control or police to report suspected abuse. If an animal is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

  • United States: Call 911 for immediate danger. For other reports, contact local animal control or police; Humane World for Animals’ reporting guide has more information.
  • Canada: Contact your local animal control agency, SPCA, or police. Call 911 for immediate danger.
  • England and Wales: Call the RSPCA cruelty line at 0300 1234 999.
  • Scotland: Call the Scottish SPCA helpline at 03000 999 999 (open daily, 9 a.m.–5 p.m.).
  • Elsewhere: Contact your local animal welfare authority or police; call your local emergency number if the animal is in immediate danger.
